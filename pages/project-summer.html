summer cicadas

夏蟬

lisle, il | june 2024

I went to Morton Arboretum to spot the 17-year periodic cicadas. They were born in 2007, and now, after 17 years, they emerge from the ground, reaching the end of their lives. Are we also like cicadas, born among billions of others, making noise at a certain frequency before crashing from trees just before the arrival of summer?

This year, the 13-year brood will also co-emerge with the 17-year brood slightly south in Illinois. Both have prime-numbered life cycles, creating a convergence that happens once every 221 years. It’s a long time, surpassing even the miracles of auroras or solar eclipses. Way more romantic.
